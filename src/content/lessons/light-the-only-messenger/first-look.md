Nobody has touched a star. Apart from a few rocks and some dust brought back from very nearby, everything we know about space came to us as light, and had to be read from it.

Light is fast, but space is big enough that even light takes its time.

- From the Moon: a little over 1 second
- From the Sun: 8 minutes
- From the nearest other star: more than 4 years
- From the Andromeda Galaxy: about 2.5 million years

So you never see the sky as it is. You see the Sun as it was 8 minutes ago. On a dark night you can see the Andromeda Galaxy as a faint smudge, and that light set out before there were any people.

Looking further away always means looking further back. It is the nearest thing to a time machine that anyone will ever have.
