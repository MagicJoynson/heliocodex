Venus, Earth and Mars are neighbours, and all three are made of rock. But you could only live on one of them.

Venus is hot enough to melt lead. Mars is colder than Antarctica. The Earth is in between.

Distance from the Sun is part of the reason, but only part. The bigger reason is air.

Some gases in the air work like a blanket. They let sunshine in and stop some of the warmth from getting back out. This is called the greenhouse effect.

Venus has a blanket that is far too thick. Mars has almost none. The Earth's is just thick enough to keep the oceans from freezing.
