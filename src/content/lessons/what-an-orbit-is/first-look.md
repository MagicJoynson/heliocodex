Throw a ball and it curves down to the ground. Throw it harder and it lands further away. Isaac Newton asked what would happen if you kept going.

Picture a cannon on a mountain so high that it stands above the air. Fire a ball level with the ground and it falls in a long curve. Fire it faster and it lands further round the world.

Now fire it at about 28,000 km/h. The ball still falls, but the Earth is round, and at that speed the ground curves away beneath the ball exactly as fast as the ball drops towards it. It never gets any closer. It goes all the way round and would hit the cannon from behind.

That is an orbit: falling, and missing, for ever.

Nothing holds the Moon up. It is falling round the Earth, and the Earth is falling round the Sun.
