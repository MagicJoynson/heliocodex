Push a child on a swing at just the right moment each time and a small push goes a long way. Push at random and not much happens.

Moons can push each other like that. Three of Jupiter's big moons do it all the time.

  <figcaption><b>Real photographs, arranged.</b> Jupiter's four big moons, with their sizes to scale: Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto, in order outward. The first three keep step. Callisto does not. Pictures by NASA's Galileo spacecraft. Credit: NASA/JPL/DLR.</figcaption>
</figure>

Io is the closest in and the quickest. In the time it takes Ganymede to go round Jupiter once, Europa goes round twice and Io goes round four times. They have kept this up for a very long time.

<figure class="photo">
  <img src="/img/learn/charts/three-moons-in-step.svg" alt="Three bars of the same length: Io split into four laps, Europa into two and Ganymede as one." width="800" height="430" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption><b>A chart.</b> Laps round Jupiter in the same seven days. The match is close but not perfect. Io finishes its four laps about two hours before Ganymede finishes one (worked out here from the periods in NASA's JPL Horizons service).</figcaption>
</figure>

Because of that rhythm, the moons pass each other at the same places on every lap, and each gets a little tug in the same direction again and again. The tugs keep their orbits slightly oval. That is the reason Io is squeezed and heated until it erupts.

<figure class="photo">
  <img src="/img/learn/io-galileo-1999.jpg" alt="Io as a yellow, orange and pale green globe dotted with black spots and red rings." width="900" height="900" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption><b>A photograph, stitched together from many.</b> Io, from NASA's Galileo spacecraft on 3 July 1999, in colours close to what the eye would see. There are no craters from falling rocks to be seen. Lava and sulphur from its volcanoes, the dark spots, bury them too quickly. Credit: NASA/JPL/University of Arizona.</figcaption>
</figure>

When orbits keep time like this, it is called a resonance.
