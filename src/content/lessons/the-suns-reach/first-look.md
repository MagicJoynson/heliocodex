The Sun gives out more than light. It also blows a wind.

It is not a wind of air. It is a very thin stream of tiny electric particles, rushing away from the Sun in every direction at about 400 km every second.

The wind blows past all the planets. It is what pushes a comet's blue tail straight back from the Sun.

Far beyond the last planet, the wind finally runs out of push. That is where the Sun's own space ends and the space between the stars begins. The whole region inside is like a giant bubble with the Sun and planets in the middle.

Two spacecraft, Voyager 1 and Voyager 2, have flown all the way out of the bubble. They were launched in 1977 and they are still going.
