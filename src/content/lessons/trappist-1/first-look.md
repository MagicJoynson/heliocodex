About 40 light years from here there is a small, dim, red star called TRAPPIST-1. It is only a little wider than Jupiter.

Seven planets go round it. All seven are about the size of the Earth, and all are probably made of rock.

They are packed in close. The nearest takes a day and a half to go round the star. The furthest takes under 19 days. All seven orbits would fit inside the orbit of Mercury with a great deal of room to spare.

Being so close to their star would roast them, except that this star is so faint. Three of the planets get about the right amount of warmth for water to stay liquid, if they have air to hold it in.

Nobody knows yet whether they do. Telescopes are trying to find out now.

No telescope can show what these planets look like. Any picture you see of them, including ours, is an informed guess.
