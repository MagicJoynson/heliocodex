Four hundred years ago Johannes Kepler spent years working through careful measurements of where Mars had been seen in the sky. He found three rules that every planet obeys.

**One.** A planet's path is not a circle. It is a slightly squashed circle called an ellipse, and the Sun is not in the middle but a little to one side.

**Two.** A planet speeds up as it comes nearer the Sun and slows down as it moves away.

**Three.** The further a planet is from the Sun, the longer its year, and not only because it has further to go. It also moves more slowly. Mercury goes round in 88 days. Neptune takes 165 years.

Kepler did not know why these rules worked. That took Isaac Newton, about seventy years later, who showed that all three follow from gravity.
