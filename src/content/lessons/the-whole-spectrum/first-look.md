A rainbow shows every colour your eyes can see, from red on one side to violet on the other.

But the rainbow does not really stop at its edges. It carries on in both directions, with kinds of light that eyes cannot pick up.

Beyond the red end is infrared. You cannot see it, but you can feel it: it is the warmth on your face from a fire. Further on are the radio waves that carry music to a radio.

Beyond the violet end is ultraviolet, which gives you sunburn. Further still are X-rays, which doctors use to see bones.

These are all light. Stars and galaxies shine in every kind. Astronomers build special telescopes for each one, and each shows them things the others cannot.
