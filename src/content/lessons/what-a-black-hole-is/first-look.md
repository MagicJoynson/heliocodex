Throw a ball up and it falls back. Throw it faster and it goes higher. Throw it at 11 km every second and it never comes back at all: it has escaped the Earth.

Now imagine squeezing the Earth smaller while keeping all of its mass. The pull at the surface grows, and the speed you need to escape grows with it. Squeeze hard enough and the speed you need becomes the speed of light.

Nothing can travel faster than light. So from an object like that, nothing can leave. No light comes out, which is why it is black, and anything that falls in stays in, which is why it is a hole.

Black holes are real. Some are made when very heavy stars die. One with the mass of 4 million Suns sits at the centre of our own galaxy.

They do not suck things in from far away. If the Sun were swapped for a black hole of the same mass, the Earth would go cold and dark, but it would carry on round its orbit exactly as before.
