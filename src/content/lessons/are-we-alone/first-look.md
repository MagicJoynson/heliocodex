Is there life anywhere else? It is one of the oldest questions people have asked, and the honest answer today is that nobody knows.

So far, life is known on exactly one world: this one. No sign of living things has been confirmed anywhere else, and no message from anyone has ever been picked up.

That is not because we have looked everywhere. We have barely started. The space we could search has been compared to all the Earth's oceans, and what we have searched so far to a bathtub of water.

All life on Earth needs liquid water, so that is the first thing to look for. Mars had rivers and lakes long ago. Several moons of Jupiter and Saturn have whole oceans hidden under their ice. And among the thousands of planets found round other stars, some are at the right distance from their star for water to stay liquid.
